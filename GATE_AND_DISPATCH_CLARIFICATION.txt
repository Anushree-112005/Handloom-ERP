================================================================================
                    DINESH EXPORTS TEXTILE ERP SYSTEM
          WORKFLOW CLARIFICATION: GATE & DISPATCH VS. GATE & SECURITY
================================================================================

This document provides a clear explanation and operational direction to distinguish
between the two modules that contain "Gate" or "Dispatch" in their names. 

Although both deal with items leaving or entering the premises, they serve entirely
different business roles, users, and departments.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CORE DIFFERENCE AT A GLANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DETAILED MODULE BREAKDOWN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### MODULE A: Fabric Production Desk > "Gate & Dispatch"
Located under: `PROCESSING / PRODUCTION` -> `Fabric Production Desk` -> `Gate & Dispatch`
Route: `/fabric/transaction/gate`

This is a shipping preparation desk for finished fabrics.
- **New Goods Release Advice (GRA)**: Authorizes the warehouse/store to release a specific fabric batch for dispatch.
- **Cloth Gate Pass Entry**: A document listing the specific cloth rolls, bale count, and fabric weight being prepared for dispatch. This is handed to the truck driver.
- **Sales Invoice**: The commercial tax invoice for the fabric being sold.
- **E-Invoice / E-Way Bill**: The government-mandated digital transportation bill for moving the fabric goods to the customer.

*Workflow Flow*:
Fabric Inspected (Fabric Checking) -> Packed into Bales (Lot & Bale) -> Released for Shipping (Gate & Dispatch) -> Loaded onto truck.

---

### MODULE B: Main Sidebar > "Gate & Security"
Located under: `GATE & SECURITY` (Main Sidebar Menu)
Routes: `/gate/inward`, `/gate/outward`, `/gate/pass`, `/gate/reports`

This is a physical control checkpoint at the factory's physical gate.
- **Gate Inward**: Logs every single truck/vehicle entering the factory. It records the driver's name, vehicle number, arrival time, and the material type (e.g., Yarn boxes, Coal for boilers, Spare parts, Chemical drums, Stationary).
- **Gate Outward**: Logs every vehicle leaving the factory yard, verifying that they have a signed gate pass.
- **Gate Pass Creation**: General security passes for employees leaving early, contractors bringing tools, or visitors entering the facility.
- **Gate Reports**: General security audit reports on vehicle delays, peak traffic hours, and visitor logs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW THEY CONNECT IN THE REAL WORKFLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

When a shipment of finished fabric leaves the factory:
1. The **Dispatch Executive** uses the **Fabric Production Desk (Gate & Dispatch)** to create a **Cloth Gate Pass** and generate the **Sales Invoice** / **E-Way Bill**.
2. The loaded truck drives to the physical exit gate of the factory.
3. The **Security Guard** at the gate stops the truck, checks the paperwork, and logs the exit in the **Gate & Security (Gate Outward)** register, matching the vehicle number and security token.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DESIGN AND DEVELOPMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep these two directories/files separate:
  - Keep `FabricTransaction.jsx` (which contains fabric-specific `gate_pass` and `einvoice_eway` structures) in the `src/pages/cloth` folder.
  - Keep the generic security screens (`GateInward.jsx`, `GateOutward.jsx`, `GatePass.jsx`, `GateReports.jsx`) in the `src/pages/gate` folder.
- Do not merge them, as security guards should never have access to commercial sales invoices or fabric grading details, and dispatch planners do not need to log coal delivery vehicles.
