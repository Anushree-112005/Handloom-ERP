3: [h] Main Functions
4: [list] Party categorization: Classify parties (Sales, Purchase, Agent, Vendor, Processor, etc.) for easy filtering and reporting.Business details entry: Capture company name, address, region, and contact information.Tax information management: Record GSTIN, PAN, TIN, CST, and GST type for compliance.Financial tracking: Manage credit days, credit limits, tally numbers, and payment terms.Contact management: Store phone numbers, emails, and point-of-contact details.Logistics integration: Include delivery party names, transport details, and delivery addresses.Role assignment: Assign responsibility to Merchandisers, Managers, and Accounts Incharge.
