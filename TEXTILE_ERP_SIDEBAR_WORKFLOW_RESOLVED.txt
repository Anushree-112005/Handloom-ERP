================================================================================
                    DINESH EXPORTS TEXTILE ERP SYSTEM
              SIDEBAR NAVIGATION & WORKFLOW RESOLUTION GUIDE
================================================================================

This document maps out the unified, workflow-oriented navigation structure for 
Dinesh Exports Textile ERP. It outlines the exact modules, menus, and sub-pages 
as they should appear in the sidebar, resolves all duplicate/confusing links, 
and explains what pages have been consolidated, removed, or redirected.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 8-STAGE WORKFLOW SEQUENCE (ORDER OF ENTRIES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent confusion, the system is organized sequentially according to the real 
textile export operations lifecycle:

Stage 1 → Setup & Masters (Company Settings, User, Party & Employee Masters)
Stage 2 → Order Management & Planning (Buyer Orders & Work Orders)
Stage 3 → Design & Development (Fabric Designs & Specifications)
Stage 4 → Material Procurement (Yarn Purchase Orders & Yarn Inward)
Stage 5 → Warping, Weaving & Greige (Beam Entries, Sizing & Greige Receipts)
Stage 6 → Processing & Production (Dyeing, Fabric checking & Lot/Bale Packing)
Stage 7 → Quality Control & Audit (Final finished cloth inspection grids)
Stage 8 → Sales, Gate & Accounts (GRA, Sales Invoices, Eway Bills, LC Details)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. DUPLICATE & CONFUSING LINKS RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following duplicate links have been resolved to streamline the user interface:

*   [DUPLICATE] Warp Beam Received & Warp Beam Delivery Entry:
    - Status: REMOVED from the main sidebar.
    - Solution: Merged into the central "Warping/Sizing Transaction" desk under the
      "Beam & Transaction Entries" tab. Accessing it via the desk prevents screen bloat.

*   [DUPLICATE] Sales Invoice vs. Direct Sales Invoice:
    - Status: SEPARATED BY ROLE.
    - Solution:
      1. Primary Entry: "Sales Invoice" (`/sales-invoice`) inside the Sales & Dispatch sidebar menu.
      2. Shortcut Link: A card inside "Fabric Production Desk > Gate & Dispatch" redirects here.
      3. Financial Accounting: "Direct Sales Invoice" & "Export Invoice" are managed in the
         "Accounts & Finance > Account Transaction > Sales Invoices" desk for tax/tally sync.

*   [DUPLICATE] On-Table Checking (Finished) vs. ON Table Checking (Greige):
    - Status: RETAINED BUT DIFFERENTIATED.
    - Solution:
      1. Finished Fabric QC: "On-Table Checking" (`/cloth/checking`) belongs to Quality Control module.
      2. Greige/Grey Cloth QC: "ON Table Checking" belongs to the "Greige Transactions > Greige Operations" tab.
      They process different fabrics (greige raw fabric vs. finished dyed fabric).

*   [DUPLICATE] Goods Release (GRA) vs. New Goods Release Advice:
    - Status: MERGED.
    - Solution: Standalone sidebar item "Goods Release (GRA)" (`/goods-release`) is the primary view.
      The card link in the Fabric Production desk is a redirect to `/goods-release`.

*   [DUPLICATE] E-Way Bill vs. Cloth Delivery Eway Bill:
    - Status: CONSOLIDATED.
    - Solution: The standalone menu "E-Way Bill" (`/eway-bill`) in Export & Logistics is the primary page.
      Greige/Fabric modules contain direct links to this page.

*   [DUPLICATE] Log Report:
    - Status: CONSOLIDATED.
    - Solution: Removed from the "Sub Masters > System Config & Utilities" list.
      Retained only in the "Reports & MIS" section as a main menu item.

4. KEY CLARIFICATION: GATE & DISPATCH (FABRIC DESK) VS. GATE & SECURITY (MAIN GATE)
================================================================================

- "Gate & Dispatch" (under Fabric Production Desk):
  * Department: Sales / Warehouse / Dispatch
  * Purpose: Fabrication preparation, commercial documentation (invoices, fabric gate passes, e-way bills) specifically for outbound fabric orders.
  * Role: Logistics planning and commercial validation of fabric roll loads.

- "Gate & Security" (Main Sidebar Section):
  * Department: Security Checkpoint
  * Purpose: Security check-in/check-out for all incoming/outgoing trucks (yarn, coal, chemical supplies, spare parts) and general gate pass logs.
  * Role: Ground security logging of vehicle numbers, entry times, and gate passes.

For maximum clarity and role-based access control, keep these modules isolated from one another.
