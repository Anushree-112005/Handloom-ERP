================================================================================
                    DINESH EXPORTS - TEXTILE EXPORT ERP
                   COMPLETE SEQUENCE & WORKFLOW BLUEPRINT
================================================================================

This blueprint defines the absolute step-by-step sequential operational flow
for the Dinesh Exports ERP system. It lists the exact modules, menus, and pages 
in the precise order they must be populated, configured, and processed to achieve 
full end-to-end data integrity from baseline configuration to commercial billing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                  SUMMARY OF THE 5 MAJOR SEGMENTS OF THE ERP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE FOUNDATION SEGMENT (Configuration of Masters & Sub-Masters)
   - Establish baseline drop-downs, parameters, tax brackets, and rules.
2. THE COMMERCIAL INITIATION (Design specs & Buyer commitments)
   - Log fabrics technical parameters, create designs, and book buyer orders.
3. THE RAW INGREDIENT CYCLE (Yarn purchasing, gate inwards, and yarn storage)
   - Purchase raw cotton or dyed yarns, check gate weight, and verify warehouse stock.
4. THE PROCESSING & JOB WORK CYCLE (Preparatory mills, warping, weaving, & mending)
   - Send yarn for dyeing, twist yarns, warp sizing, weave into Greige fabric,
     piece checking, and mending.
5. THE FULFILLMENT & FINANCIALS (Logistics, dispatch, invoice, and ledger receipt)
   - Formulate despatch plans, pack rolls, generate lorry indents, clear gate entry, 
     generate sales tax invoice, and book payment vouchers.

================================================================================
                 THE SEQUENTIAL BLUEPRINT (STEP 1 TO STEP 40)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 1] - BASELINE SYSTEM CONFIGURATIONS (SUB-MASTERS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* FIRST: Core Drops setup
  - Purpose: Establish currency notations, physical sizes, and core types.
  - STEP 1: Currency Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/currency_master`
    * Action: Populate currency symbols (e.g., INR - ₹, USD - $, EUR - €)
  - STEP 2: Unit Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/unit_master`
    * Action: Log standard measurements (e.g. MTR, ROLL, KG, BAG, PCS)
  - STEP 3: Yarn Type Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/yarn_type_master`
    * Action: Record fibers (e.g. Cotton, Polyester, Linen, Viscose, Lycra)
  - STEP 4: Yarn Count Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/yarn_count_master`
    * Action: Log counts (e.g. 20s, 30s, 40s, 2/40s, 60s Compact Yarn)
  - STEP 5: Color Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/color_master`
    * Action: Record standard shades and Pantone color identifiers.

* SECOND: Plant Operations setup
  - Purpose: Define physical logistics points, storage rooms, and equipment.
  - STEP 6: Gate Location Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/gate_location_master`
    * Action: Register security entries (e.g. Main Gate, Inward Terminal 1)
  - STEP 7: Godown Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/godown_master`
    * Action: Configure inventory storage bays (e.g. Grey Warehouse, Yarn Store)
  - STEP 8: Mill Name Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/mill_name_master`
    * Action: Add partner spinning mills (e.g. Vardhman Spinning, Premier Mills)
  - STEP 9: Loom Master & Loom Type Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/loom_master` and `/sub-master/loom_type_master`
    * Action: Register mechanical loom configurations (e.g. Airjet, Rapier, Sulzer)
  - STEP 10: Process Type Master & Process Sequences Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/process_type_master` & `/sub-master/process_sequences_master`
    * Action: Map operational routing states (e.g. Weaving $\rightarrow$ Dyeing $\rightarrow$ Sanforizing)

* THIRD: Financial & Administrative baselines
  - Purpose: Set billing tax rules, locations, and groups.
  - STEP 11: HSN Code Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/hsn_code_master`
    * Action: Enter official HSN codes and corresponding GST percentage tax rates.
  - STEP 12: Payment Terms Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/payment_terms_master`
    * Action: Define commercial terms (e.g. 30 Days Credit, 100% Advance)
  - STEP 13: District & City Master & Sales Region Master
    * Page: `/sub-master/district_city_master` & `/sub-master/sales_region_master`
    * Action: Formulate shipping zones and regions for logistical coverage.

* FOURTH: Register Core Actors
  - Purpose: Log external companies and internal operators.
  - STEP 14: Party Master
    * Page: `/party-master` (PartyMaster.jsx)
    * Action: Log full profiles for Buyers, Spinning Mill Suppliers, and Jobwork Weavers.
  - STEP 15: Employee Master
    * Page: `/employee-master` (EmployeeMaster.jsx)
    * Action: Log staff directories, designating supervisors, security heads, and QC checkers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 2] - COMMERCIAL COMMITTAL & FABRIC DESIGNS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Document fabric technical specifications and book official buyer POs.

* STEP 16: Technical Design Entry
  - Menu: Masters -> Design Entry
  - Page: `/design-entry` (DesignEntry.jsx)
  - Action: Record detailed specifications for fabric (GSM, Cuttable Width,
    Reed/Pick counts, weave structure type) and attach reference image.
  - Why: This creates the master recipe for weaving looms.

* STEP 17: Log Buyer Orders (IBPO)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Buyer Order
  - Page: `/buyer-order` (BuyerOrder.jsx)
  - Action: Formally book the commercial order. Specify Order Date, Party Name (from Step 14),
    Agent, delivery address, transport terms, and map fabric designs (from Step 16)
    with total ordered meters, price rates, and ex-factory target dates.
  - Why: Generates the unique `IBPO-XXXXX` identifier which is referenced by all down-stream steps.

* STEP 18: Design Create Work Voucher
  - Menu: Work Order -> Transactions -> Design & Development
  - Page: `/work-order/transactions` -> Design Create Tab (WorkOrderDesk.jsx)
  - Action: Generate a design create ledger transaction mapping the fabric construction 
    parameters to initiate experimental sample looms.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 3] - RAW INGREDIENT PROCUREMENTS (YARN & GREIGE ACCESS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Buy and stock the required yarns or grey fabric to fulfill the orders.

* STEP 19: Yarn Purchase Orders (YPO)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Yarn Purchase Order
  - Page: `/yarn-po` (YarnPurchaseOrder.jsx)
  - Action: Select spinning mill name, payment terms, and list count specifications 
    (e.g., 40s combed cotton, quantity in bags, rate per kg, total value).
  - Why: Formally issues the purchasing contract to spinning mills.

* STEP 20: Inward Yarn at Mill Gate
  - Menu: Transactions -> Yarn Inward
  - Page: `/yarn-inward` (YarnInward.jsx)
  - Action: Log incoming trucks at Gate Terminal. Verify weight, package count, 
    spinning mill invoice reference number, and allocate storage bin in godown.
  - Why: Updates raw yarn physical stock inventory.

* STEP 21: Grey Yarn Delivery (To Job Workers)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Grey Yarn Delivery
  - Page: `/grey-yarn-delivery` (GreyYarnDelivery.jsx)
  - Action: Issue yarn rolls out of Godowns to Preparatory Sizing or Weaving mills.
  - Why: Logs material shift from raw inventory to Work-in-Progress (WIP) stock.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 4] - JOB WORK & MANUFACTURING (THE PROCESS PIPELINE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Coordinate dyed material, preparatory beam sizing, and weaving looms.

* STEP 22: Dyed Yarn Receipt (If coloring is contracted)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Dyed Yarn Received
  - Page: `/dyed-yarn-received` (DyedYarnReceived.jsx)
  - Action: Record colored yarn batches returning from external Dyehouse vendors.

* STEP 23: Issue Warping & Sizing Beam Orders
  - Menu: Work Order -> Transactions -> Yarn & Fabric Prep
  - Page: `/work-order/transactions` -> Warping/Sizing Order Tab
  - Action: Log beams setup parameters (Total Ends, Warp Length in meters, Beam counts) 
    essential for structural durability on weaving machines.

* STEP 24: Warp Beam Receipt (Beams returning to factory)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Warp Beam Receipt
  - Page: `/warp-beam-receipt` (WarpBeamReceipt.jsx)
  - Action: Receipt warp beam arrays returning from sizing mills to be forwarded to looms.

* STEP 25: Issue Weaving Jobwork Orders
  - Menu: Work Order -> Transactions -> Order Management
  - Page: `/work-order/transactions` -> Vendor Order Tab
  - Action: Contract jobwork weaving parties to weave yarns into Greige cloth.

* STEP 26: Cloth Inward Entry (Fabric Arrives)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Cloth Inward
  - Page: `/cloth-inward` (ClothInward.jsx)
  - Action: Receive freshly woven grey fabric rolls arriving at godowns. Log weaver code, 
    roll numbers, piece meter lengths, and lot weights.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 5] - QUALITY CONTROL & STOCK CONVERSIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Thorough inspection, mending, and warehouse packing.

* STEP 27: On-Table Defect Checking
  - Menu: Transactions -> On-Table Checking
  - Page: `/cloth-checking` (OnTableChecking.jsx)
  - Action: Complete piece-by-piece roll checks. Log warp lines, weft cracks,
    or dirt defects. Calculate the automated point-based fabric Grade (Grade A, B, or C).

* STEP 28: Finished Fabric Inward
  - Menu: Transactions -> Finished Fabric Inward
  - Page: `/finished-fabrics` (FinishedFabricInward.jsx)
  - Action: Record rolls arriving from commercial Dyeing/Processing/Sanforizing mills, 
    mapping chemical parameters and finished color codes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 6] - DESPATCH PLANNING & PACKING FULFILLMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Shipments scheduling, roll manifest wrapping, and transport coordination.

* STEP 29: Despatch Planning anchor
  - Menu: Transactions -> Despatch Planning
  - Page: `/despatch-planning` (DespatchPlanning.jsx)
  - Action: Pull all unfulfilled Buyer Orders (IBPO). Group rolls, select shipping 
    routes, set targets, and assign packaging priorities.

* STEP 30: Packing Slip generation
  - Menu: Transactions -> Packing Slip
  - Page: `/packing-slip` (PackingSlip.jsx)
  - Action: Wrap rolls into bales/cartons. Specify bale number, weight, roll lists 
    contained within, and print the package shipping label manifest.

* STEP 31: Goods Release Advice (GRA)
  - Menu: Transactions -> Goods Release
  - Page: `/goods-releases` (GoodsRelease.jsx)
  - Action: Issue technical clearance authorizing warehouse loaders to fetch bales 
    for loading onto transporter trucks.

* STEP 32: Dispatch Indents
  - Menu: Transactions -> Dispatch & Expense
  - Page: `/dispatch-expense` -> Dispatch Indent View (DispatchExpenseSubModule.jsx)
  - Action: Log Lorry details, transporter agent names, LR numbers, vehicle plates, 
    and target delivery place.

* STEP 33: Log Freight & Loading Expenses
  - Menu: Transactions -> Dispatch & Expense
  - Page: `/dispatch-expense` -> Expense Entry View
  - Action: Record transport charges, fuel, loading expenses, or customs freight.

* STEP 34: Statutory E-Way Bills
  - Menu: Transactions -> E-Way Bill Entry
  - Page: `/eway-bill` (EwayBill.jsx)
  - Action: Populate tax details, calculate SGST/CGST or IGST, log transporter distance, 
    and simulate digital tax portal token clearance.

* STEP 35: Gate Outward clearing
  - Menu: Transactions -> Gate Pass (or Security Outward)
  - Page: `/gate-pass` (GatePass.jsx)
  - Action: Generate gate pass sheet. Verify driver name, check loader cargo manifest, 
    and record the timestamp exiting factory premises.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 7] - SALES TAX INVOICING & PAYMENT RECEIPTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Goal: Convert physical dispatches into financial ledgers and finalize accounts.

* STEP 36: Commercial Sales Invoice
  - Menu: Transactions -> Sales Invoice
  - Page: `/sales-invoice` (SalesInvoice.jsx)
  - Action: Pull completed packing slips. Auto-apply regional GST brackets based on HSN code. 
    Submit to generate commercial legal Invoice.
  - Why: Creates the accounts receivable debt on the buyer's company profile ledger.

* STEP 37: Financial Voucher Entries
  - Menu: Transactions -> Voucher Entry
  - Page: `/voucher-entry` (VoucherEntry.jsx)
  - Action: Record payment receipts from buyer bank transfers to balance the sales invoice.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[STAGE 8] - AUDITS, COMPLIANCES & SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* STEP 38: Check System Log Reports
  - Menu: Transactions -> Log Reports
  - Page: `/log-reports` (LogReports.jsx)
  - Action: Review user action logs to keep audit trails secure.

* STEP 39: General Company Settings
  - Menu: System Utilities -> Company Settings
  - Page: `/company-settings` (CompanySettings.jsx)
  - Action: Configure base export registration, bank configurations, and logo.

* STEP 40: Overview & Dashboard analytics
  - Menu: Dashboard -> Overview
  - Page: `/overview` (Overview.jsx)
  - Action: Check real-time progress charts, pending dispatches, and sales reports.
